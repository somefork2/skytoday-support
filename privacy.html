Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Sky Today computes the sky on your device. We don’t know who you are: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking. To improve the app we collect anonymous usage statistics, described below.

What we collect

Sky Today uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used: which screens are opened, which features are tried (for example the finder or the hologram card), whether onboarding is finished, and which plan is chosen when someone subscribes. Along with these events Firebase records technical data such as the device model, iOS version, app version, app language, country and a random app-instance identifier. We also record the broad latitude band of the chosen place (for example “northern” or “tropics”), never coordinates or a city name.

This data is not linked to your name, email or Apple Account, is not used for advertising and is not shared with data brokers. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not track you across other apps or websites. Google processes the data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms; statistics are kept for up to 14 months.

We do not run servers of our own that receive your data.

Location

If you allow it, your approximate location is used on the device to work out which planets are above your horizon and when they rise and set. It is stored only in the app’s private storage and shared with the app’s own widgets and Apple Watch app. To show a place name, iOS looks up the coordinates with Apple’s geocoding service; when you search for a city, the search goes to Apple Maps. These requests are handled by Apple under its privacy policy.

Camera and motion

The finder shows the live camera picture and reads the device’s orientation so it can label the sky. The picture and the motion data are used on the spot and are never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Sky data

Once in a while the app downloads a public data file (comets, asteroid flybys, satellite orbits, aurora forecast) from GitHub Pages. The request contains no personal information. As with any website, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Notifications

If you allow them, event alerts and the reminder before a free trial ends are scheduled locally on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Sky Today does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The anonymous usage statistics above are the same for every user.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
