Sky Today tells you what is in the sky tonight and where to look: visible planets, Moon phases, eclipses for your place, meteor showers, retrograde loops explained, comets, satellite passes and an AR finder.

Questions and feedback

The finder points the wrong way

Calibrate the compass: move the phone in a figure 8 a few times, away from metal and magnets. Phone cases with magnets can also confuse the compass.

Times look wrong

Check the place in Settings. Times are shown in the time zone of the chosen place.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap “Restore purchases” in the app.

Accuracy

Positions are computed on the device with VSOP87 and ELP-2000 theories and checked against NASA JPL DE440: planets to about a second of arc, eclipse and Moon phase times to under a minute.
